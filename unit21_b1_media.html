الكتابة

اكتب عن الإعلام والصحافة
What makes a news story trustworthy? How do you evaluate sources?
ما الذي يجعل خبراً جديراً بالثقة؟ كيف تقيّم المصادر؟
A trustworthy news story is one in which multiple independent sources have been cited, claims have been verified, and corrections are published when errors are made. The journalist's identity and the outlet's editorial standards should be transparent. When a story is being reported by several credible organisations simultaneously, its reliability is significantly increased.
How has social media changed journalism and the way news is consumed?
كيف غيّرت وسائل التواصل الاجتماعي الصحافة وطريقة استهلاك الأخبار؟
News is now being consumed in real-time, which has dramatically accelerated the publication cycle. Stories are being shared before they have been properly verified, and corrections are rarely seen by those who viewed the original error. The traditional gatekeeping role of editors has been largely bypassed by social media algorithms.
Is complete journalistic objectivity possible? Should journalists express opinions?
هل الموضوعية الصحفية الكاملة ممكنة؟ هل يجب على الصحفيين التعبير عن آرائهم؟
Complete objectivity cannot be achieved, as every editorial decision involves a degree of judgment. However, news reporting should be clearly distinguished from opinion journalism. Opinions should be labelled transparently so that readers can assess the information they are being given with appropriate critical awareness.
What are the dangers of disinformation and how can it be countered?
ما مخاطر التضليل الإعلامي وكيف يمكن مواجهته؟
Disinformation is being spread faster than corrections can be issued, which makes it extraordinarily dangerous to democratic processes. False narratives, once established, are extremely difficult to displace. Media literacy education needs to be prioritised, and platforms should be required to take greater responsibility for the content being published on them.
Should freedom of the press ever be limited? Under what circumstances?
هل يجب أن تُقيَّد حرية الصحافة أحياناً؟ في أي ظروف؟
A free press is regarded as essential to democracy and should be protected vigorously. Limitations can be justified only in narrow circumstances — when national security is genuinely threatened or when privacy rights are being violated without public interest justification. Governments should never be permitted to restrict journalism merely because criticism is being directed at those in power.
