الكتابة

اكتب عن العلوم والاكتشاف
If you had studied science at university, what field would you be working in now?
لو كنت درست العلوم في الجامعة في أي مجال كنت ستعمل الآن؟
If I had studied science at university, I would probably be working in neuroscience now. The brain has always fascinated me, and if I had pursued that path, I might be contributing to research on memory and learning.
What scientific discovery do you think has most changed human life?
ما الاكتشاف العلمي الذي تعتقد أنه غيّر الحياة البشرية أكثر؟
I believe the discovery of antibiotics has most transformed human life. If Fleming had not discovered penicillin in 1928, millions of people who survived infections would have died. Modern surgery would be impossible without this foundation.
Do you think science has solved more problems than it has created? Why?
هل تعتقد أن العلوم حلّت مشاكل أكثر مما خلقت؟ لماذا؟
On balance, I think science has solved more problems than it has created. If scientists had not developed vaccines, billions would have suffered preventable deaths. However, if nuclear technology had been managed more responsibly, we would not be living with the threat of weapons of mass destruction today.
Describe a scientific topic you find fascinating and explain why.
صف موضوعاً علمياً تجده رائعاً واشرح لماذا.
I find quantum physics absolutely fascinating. The idea that particles can exist in multiple states simultaneously challenges everything we think we know about reality. If scientists fully understood quantum mechanics, we would probably have computers millions of times more powerful than today.
If humanity had prioritised space exploration differently, where might we be today?
لو كانت البشرية أولّت استكشاف الفضاء بشكل مختلف أين كنا سنكون اليوم؟
If humanity had invested more consistently in space exploration, we might have established a permanent base on the Moon by now. If we had developed better propulsion technology earlier, Mars missions would be routine rather than experimental today.
