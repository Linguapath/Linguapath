الكتابة

اكتب عن العمل والمستقبل
What will you have achieved in your career by the time you are forty?
ما الذي ستكون قد حققته في مسيرتك بحلول بلوغك الأربعين؟
By the time I am forty I will have completed my English qualification and will have been working in my chosen field for at least ten years. I hope I will have built enough experience to be working independently. By that point I will have learned far more from failures than from successes — and I think that will have been the most valuable education of all.
How will AI have changed your industry by 2035?
كيف سيكون الذكاء الاصطناعي قد غيّر صناعتك بحلول 2035؟
By 2035 AI will have automated most of the routine tasks in my field, which means workers will need to have developed skills that machines cannot replicate — creativity, judgment, and empathy. Those who will have invested in continuous learning will be in the strongest position. The workers who will have ignored reskilling will have found themselves in a very difficult situation.
What will work look like by the time the next generation retires?
كيف سيبدو العمل بحلول تقاعد الجيل القادم؟
By the time the next generation retires they will have experienced multiple complete career changes. They will have been reskilling continuously for decades. The concept of a single employer for life will have disappeared entirely. Most people will have spent significant portions of their careers in the gig economy. Whether this will have made work more fulfilling or more precarious — I genuinely do not know.
Describe your English learning journey using Future Perfect.
صف رحلة تعلّمك للإنجليزية باستخدام المستقبل التام.
By the time I finish this B1 course I will have studied English seriously for over a year. I will have been building my vocabulary every single day. By the time I reach B2 level I will have read hundreds of texts in English and will have produced thousands of sentences. The progress will have been gradual but by that point it will have been genuinely transformative.
Do you think the four-day working week will have been widely adopted by 2035?
هل تعتقد أن أسبوع العمل من أربعة أيام سيكون قد انتشر بحلول 2035؟
By 2035 I believe the four-day week will have been adopted in many progressive companies, particularly in the technology sector. By that point researchers will have been studying its effects for over fifteen years and will have produced convincing evidence about productivity and wellbeing. However, many industries will not have changed — the resistance to reform in traditional sectors will have proven stubborn.
