الكتابة

اكتب عن اللغة وعلم اللغويات
Why are you learning English? Use nominalisations.
لماذا تتعلم الإنجليزية؟ استخدم الأسماء المشتقة.
My motivation for the acquisition of English is the realisation that it opens access to an enormous range of knowledge and opportunity. The development of a second language is not simply the development of a skill — it is the gradual construction of an alternative way of thinking. My experience has been one of frustration followed by breakthrough, repeatedly.
Does language shape how we think? Use nominalisations.
هل تُشكّل اللغة طريقة تفكيرنا؟ استخدم الأسماء المشتقة.
The relationship between language and thought is one of the most contested questions in linguistics. My observation is that the translation between Arabic and English sometimes requires the reconstruction of concepts. The existence of concepts in one language without equivalent in another suggests genuine differences in how speakers categorise experience.
What is lost when a language dies? Use nominalisations.
ما الذي يُفقد حين تموت لغة؟ استخدم الأسماء المشتقة.
The extinction of a language is the permanent destruction of a unique cognitive framework. The classification of plants, the narration of history — all shaped by the particular vocabulary of a given language. The disappearance of linguistic diversity represents an irreversible reduction in humanity's ways of understanding the world.
Discuss the global dominance of English using nominalisations.
ناقش الهيمنة العالمية للإنجليزية باستخدام الأسماء المشتقة.
The globalisation of English creates both opportunity and inequality. The expectation that all academic communication should take place in English disadvantages non-native speakers. The imposition of a single global language represents a form of cultural standardisation with genuine costs to diversity.
What is the best approach to language learning? Use nominalisations.
ما أفضل نهج لتعلم اللغة؟ استخدم الأسماء المشتقة.
The most effective approach combines immersion with explicit instruction. The development of listening comprehension must precede the production of speech. The improvement of writing requires extensive reading. Without consistent exposure to authentic language, formal study alone produces knowledge without genuine fluency.
