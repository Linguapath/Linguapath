الكتابة

اكتب عن التكنولوجيا والذكاء الاصطناعي
How do you use AI in your daily life?
كيف تستخدم الذكاء الاصطناعي في حياتك اليومية؟
She said that she used AI every day to help with writing, research, and planning. I use it similarly — mainly for drafting content and summarising long documents, which saves me considerable time.
What is the biggest benefit of technology in your work or study?
ما أكبر فائدة للتكنولوجيا في عملك أو دراستك؟
My teacher told us that technology had transformed how we access knowledge. For me, the biggest benefit is instant access to resources from anywhere — something that was impossible for previous generations.
What do you think is the greatest risk of AI?
ما أكبر خطر تراه في الذكاء الاصطناعي؟
Experts warned that AI could replace millions of jobs without adequate support for displaced workers. I believe the greatest risk is not the technology itself, but the speed of change outpacing our ability to adapt.
How do you think AI will change your professional field?
كيف تعتقد أن الذكاء الاصطناعي سيغيّر مجالك المهني؟
A researcher claimed that automation would transform education within a decade. I think AI will handle routine tasks, freeing teachers and educators to focus on creativity, mentorship, and deeper human connection.
What should governments do about AI regulation?
ما الذي يجب على الحكومات فعله بشأن تنظيم الذكاء الاصطناعي؟
She argued that governments needed to regulate AI without stifling innovation. I agree — clear ethical guidelines, transparency requirements, and independent oversight bodies should be established as a priority.
