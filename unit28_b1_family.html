الكتابة

اكتب عن الأسرة والمجتمع
Describe your family structure using ellipsis and substitution.
صف هيكل عائلتك باستخدام الحذف والإحالة.
My family is a large extended one and so are most families in my culture. Neither my parents nor my grandparents lived far from each other. I think so when people say the extended family offers something the nuclear family cannot — I have experienced it directly. Neither isolation nor loneliness was part of my childhood, and I don't think it would have been possible without that network.
What values did your family pass down? Use so/neither/nor.
ما القيم التي أورثتها عائلتك؟ استخدم so/neither/nor.
My parents valued honesty deeply and so did their parents before them. Neither money nor status was treated as the measure of a person. I think so when researchers say values are transmitted through behaviour — neither lectures nor rules taught me what I know, but the way my parents lived. Nor do I take those values for granted now that I am responsible for passing them on.
Is the traditional family model still relevant today?
هل نموذج الأسرة التقليدي لا يزال ذا صلة اليوم؟
I think so in certain respects — neither the need for security nor the need for belonging has changed. But so has society changed that the single model of family no longer fits. Neither a fixed structure nor rigid roles can accommodate the range of human circumstances. So do most families now create their own version of the institution rather than following a predetermined template.
Discuss the challenges of work-life balance for modern families.
ناقش تحديات التوازن بين العمل والحياة للأسر الحديثة.
Neither parent in a two-income household can give full attention to work and family simultaneously — nor can they avoid the guilt of this division. So have expectations of both work and parenting increased that neither is achievable at the standard previously expected. I think so when people argue that policy must change — neither market forces nor individual willpower alone can solve a structural problem.
What does family mean to you personally? Use substitution.
ما معنى الأسرة بالنسبة لك شخصياً؟ استخدم الإحالة.
Family means the people who remain when everything else falls away. I think so more strongly with every passing year. Neither wealth nor achievement means as much as I once thought it would — nor does status. So do all the people I most respect seem to have reached the same conclusion. Neither can be replaced by anything else — and I don't think we should stop reminding ourselves of that.
