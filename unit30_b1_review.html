الكتابة

اكتب عن مراجعة شاملة وشهادة B1
Reflect on your B1 journey using at least five different grammar structures.
تأمّل رحلة B1 الخاصة بك باستخدام خمسة هياكل قواعدية مختلفة على الأقل.
I have been studying English consistently for several months, and never have I felt so aware of my own progress. I wish I had started sooner — if only someone had told me earlier that grammar, although challenging, eventually clicks. It is the speaking practice that has made the biggest difference. By the time I complete this course I will have covered structures I once thought were beyond me. The development of confidence, not just accuracy, has been the real achievement.
What topic from this course surprised or interested you most?
ما الموضوع الذي فاجأك أو أثار اهتمامك أكثر من هذا الكورس؟
What surprised me most was the philosophy unit. Never had I expected to discuss whether free will exists in an English course. If only more language teaching used real intellectual content. The evidence tends to suggest that genuine curiosity accelerates acquisition — and I think so strongly after this experience.
What will you do differently now that you have completed B1?
ما الذي ستفعله بشكل مختلف الآن بعد إتمام B1؟
By this time next year I will have been studying B2 for several months. I should have started using English for real purposes much earlier. I wish I had been less afraid of making mistakes. Not only will I continue studying but I will also start using English daily outside the course.
Write a letter of advice to someone starting B1 today.
اكتب رسالة نصيحة لشخص يبدأ B1 اليوم.
Dear future B1 student — although the early units feel overwhelming, they are building something you cannot see yet. Never give up when a grammar point seems impossible — it will click eventually. What matters most is consistency. By the time you reach unit thirty you will have been building something remarkable, one day at a time.
Final Writing: What does English mean to you now compared to when you started?
الكتابة النهائية: ما معنى الإنجليزية لك الآن مقارنةً بحين بدأت؟
When I started, English was a wall. Now it is becoming a window. Never have I felt the gap between what I want to say and what I can say close so noticeably. I wish I had understood from the beginning that every uncomfortable moment of not understanding was the learning itself. The acquisition of a second language is the closest thing to acquiring a second life. By the time I reach fluency I will have become, in some small way, a different person.
