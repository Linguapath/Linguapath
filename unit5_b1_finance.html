الكتابة

اكتب عن المال والتمويل
What is your current financial goal?
ما هدفك المالي الحالي؟
If I save consistently over the next three years, I will be able to build a meaningful emergency fund and begin investing. My current goal is to reduce my monthly expenditure and redirect that money into savings.
How do you currently manage your budget?
كيف تُدير ميزانيتك حالياً؟
If I stick to my budget each month, I will reduce my debt significantly within two years. I track every expense using an app, which has made me much more aware of where my money actually goes.
What would you invest in if you had extra money?
في ماذا ستستثمر لو كان لديك مال إضافي؟
If I had extra capital, I would invest in a diversified portfolio of index funds and property. I would also keep a portion in a high-yield savings account to maintain adequate liquidity.
What would happen to your finances if you lost your job?
ماذا سيحدث لماليتك لو فقدت وظيفتك؟
If I lost my job, I would use my emergency fund to cover three months of living expenses. I would immediately reduce all non-essential expenditure and begin networking actively to find new opportunities.
What is the most important financial lesson you have learned?
ما أهم درس مالي تعلمته في حياتك؟
The most valuable lesson is that if you start saving and investing early, compound interest does most of the work for you. Time in the market consistently outperforms trying to time the market.
