الكتابة

اكتب عن المشاعر والصحة النفسية
Describe a time when you felt completely overwhelmed. What caused it?
صف وقتاً شعرت فيه بضغط شديد. ما الذي سببه؟
Feeling completely overwhelmed by the workload, I struggled to focus on even simple tasks. Having taken on too many responsibilities at once, I realised I needed to set clear boundaries. It was a situation caused largely by my inability to say no.
How do you cope when you feel anxious or stressed?
كيف تتعامل عندما تشعر بالقلق أو التوتر؟
Having learned mindfulness techniques, I now find it easier to manage anxiety. Feeling the tension building, I step away from whatever I am doing and focus on slow, deliberate breathing. It is a simple practice but one that consistently helps.
Do you think people talk enough about their emotional struggles?
هل تعتقد أن الناس يتحدثون بما يكفي عن صراعاتهم العاطفية؟
Not having been raised in a culture that values emotional expression, many people find it difficult to open up. Feeling ashamed of vulnerability, they suffer in silence. Having said that, younger generations seem far more willing to discuss mental health openly.
Describe someone you know who manages their emotions well.
صف شخصاً تعرفه يتحكم في مشاعره بشكل جيد.
Having developed remarkable self-awareness over the years, my mentor rarely reacts impulsively. Faced with criticism or conflict, she pauses, reflecting carefully before responding. Not allowing her emotions to control her actions, she consistently models the kind of emotional intelligence I aspire to.
What role does physical health play in emotional wellbeing?
ما الدور الذي تلعبه الصحة الجسدية في الصحة العاطفية؟
Having started exercising regularly, I noticed a dramatic improvement in my mood and anxiety levels. Feeling physically strong, the mind tends to follow. Research consistently shows that ignoring physical health directly undermines emotional resilience.
