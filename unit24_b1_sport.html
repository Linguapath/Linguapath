الكتابة

اكتب عن الرياضة والمنافسة
Describe a sporting achievement you are proud of using modal perfects.
صف إنجازاً رياضياً تفخر به باستخدام Modal Perfect.
I must have trained for over a thousand hours to reach that level. Looking back, I should have rested more — I might have avoided the injury that derailed my season. But the discipline I developed could not have been learned any other way.
What makes a great athlete beyond physical ability?
ما الذي يصنع الرياضي العظيم ما وراء القدرة الجسدية؟
The greatest athletes must have developed extraordinary mental resilience alongside physical skill. They cannot have reached the top without learning to manage failure constructively. Their mindset should have been their first training priority.
Should performance-enhancing drugs be banned? Argue using modal verbs.
هل يجب حظر المنشطات؟ جادل باستخدام أفعال المودال.
Performance-enhancing drugs must be banned because they cannot have been shown to be safe long-term. Athletes who use them may have won through chemical advantage rather than genuine development. The spirit of sport should have been sufficient motivation.
How does sport bring communities together?
كيف توحّد الرياضة المجتمعات؟
Sport must be one of the few arenas where people from different backgrounds cooperate toward a shared goal. The 1995 Rugby World Cup could not have done what it did without sport's unique capacity to transcend political division. Communities might have drifted further apart without the shared identity that supporting a team provides.
What can athletes teach us about handling failure?
ماذا يعلّمنا الرياضيون عن التعامل مع الفشل؟
Elite athletes must have experienced more failure than most people ever face. They cannot have reached the top without developing a relationship with defeat that ordinary life rarely forces us to build. They should have failed publicly and repeatedly — and learned each time.
