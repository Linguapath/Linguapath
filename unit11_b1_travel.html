الكتابة

اكتب عن السفر والسياحة
Describe the most memorable trip you have ever taken.
صف أكثر رحلة لا تُنسى قمت بها.
The most memorable trip I have ever taken was backpacking through Southeast Asia. I remember arriving in Bangkok and being completely overwhelmed by the noise and colour. Exploring the temples, trying street food, and meeting travellers from around the world completely changed my perspective.
What do you enjoy most about travelling? Use gerunds.
ما الذي تستمتع به أكثر في السفر؟ استخدم Gerunds.
I absolutely love exploring local markets and trying unfamiliar food. I also enjoy talking to local people and learning about their daily lives. Seeing historical sites always moves me deeply, and I find that travelling alone forces you to become more self-reliant.
What are the advantages and disadvantages of tourism for local communities?
ما مزايا وعيوب السياحة للمجتمعات المحلية؟
Tourism brings significant economic benefits — it tends to create jobs and stimulate local businesses. However, it also risks damaging cultural heritage, increasing pollution, and pricing local residents out of their own neighbourhoods. Sustainable tourism aims to balance these competing interests.
Describe a place you have always wanted to visit and explain why.
صف مكاناً طالما أردت زيارته واشرح السبب.
I have always dreamed of visiting Kyoto in Japan. I want to experience walking through ancient temples at dawn, watching the cherry blossoms fall, and learning about a culture that manages to combine deep tradition with modern innovation. I hope to go next spring.
How has travel changed you as a person?
كيف غيّرك السفر كشخص؟
Travelling has made me significantly more open-minded and adaptable. Being immersed in unfamiliar cultures taught me to stop assuming my own way of doing things is the only correct way. I also became much better at navigating uncertainty, which has helped me enormously in my professional life.
