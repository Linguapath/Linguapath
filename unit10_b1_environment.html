الكتابة

اكتب عن البيئة والمناخ
What environmental problem concerns you most and why?
ما المشكلة البيئية التي تقلقك أكثر ولماذا؟
Model: The environmental problem that concerns me most is climate change, which has been caused largely by decades of industrial emissions. I find it particularly alarming because its effects are already being felt globally.
What changes have you had made in your home to reduce your carbon footprint?
ما التغييرات التي أجريتها في منزلك لتقليل بصمتك الكربونية؟
Model: I have had solar panels installed on our roof and I have had old appliances replaced with energy-efficient ones. I also get my car serviced regularly to reduce emissions.
Do you think individuals or governments are more responsible for fixing environmental problems?
هل تعتقد أن الأفراد أم الحكومات أكثر مسؤولية عن حل المشاكل البيئية؟
Model: In my opinion, governments bear greater responsibility because they have the power to get legislation passed and have policies enforced on a national scale. Individuals alone cannot solve systemic problems.
Describe one simple change anyone could make to help the environment.
صف تغييراً بسيطاً يمكن لأي شخص القيام به لمساعدة البيئة.
Model: One of the most effective changes is getting a reusable water bottle made rather than buying plastic ones. This single habit can prevent hundreds of plastic bottles from ending up in landfill each year.
What would you like to see done about deforestation in the next ten years?
ما الذي تودّ رؤيته يتم بشأن إزالة الغابات خلال العشر سنوات القادمة؟
Model: I would like to see international agreements signed and reforestation programmes funded on a massive scale. I also want corporations held accountable and have their supply chains audited for environmental damage.
