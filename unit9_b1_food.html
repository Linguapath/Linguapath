الكتابة

اكتب عن عاداتك الغذائية وتغذيتك
Describe your typical daily diet. What do you eat and drink?
صف نظامك الغذائي اليومي المعتاد.
I consume a great deal of vegetables and wholegrains and very little processed food. A considerable amount of my diet consists of plant-based protein, and I drink far more water than most people I know.
What changes would you make to eat more healthily?
ما التغييرات التي ستُجريها لتأكل بشكل أكثر صحة؟
I would reduce the amount of saturated fat in my diet and eat far more fermented foods to support my gut microbiome. I would also consume much less sugar, which research links to inflammation and energy crashes.
Do you think people in your country have a healthy diet?
هل الناس في بلدك يتبعون نظاماً غذائياً صحياً؟
A considerable number of people rely heavily on processed food, while very few consume the recommended amount of dietary fibre. However, a growing number of younger people are switching to more plant-based options.
Describe a traditional dish from your culture and its nutritional value.
صف طبقاً تقليدياً من ثقافتك وقيمته الغذائية.
This dish contains a significant amount of legumes, which provide a great deal of plant-based protein and fibre. Very little saturated fat is used in its preparation, making it one of the most nutritionally balanced meals in our cuisine.
What is the relationship between diet and mental health in your opinion?
ما العلاقة بين النظام الغذائي والصحة النفسية؟
A great deal of research suggests that eating a diet rich in omega-3s, fibre, and fermented foods positively affects mood and cognitive function. The gut-brain axis means that what we eat directly influences how we feel and think.
