الكتابة

اكتب عن الجريمة والعدالة
Describe a crime or injustice that affected your community.
صف جريمة أو ظلم أثّر في مجتمعك.
The crime that affected our community most was a series of fraudulent schemes which targeted elderly residents. It was a case where the perpetrator, who was trusted by many, exploited that trust systematically over years.
What do you think is the most effective way to reduce crime?
ما أكثر طريقة فعّالة لتقليل الجريمة؟
The approach that works best is one that combines strong rehabilitation programmes with early intervention in deprived communities. Countries which invest in education and mental health consistently report lower crime rates.
Do you think punishment or rehabilitation is more important?
هل تعتقد أن العقوبة أم إعادة التأهيل أكثر أهمية؟
Rehabilitation, which focuses on changing behaviour and addressing root causes, produces better long-term outcomes. High recidivism rates in purely punitive systems suggest that punishment alone does not prevent reoffending.
Describe a law that you think is unfair or needs changing.
صف قانوناً تعتقد أنه غير عادل أو يحتاج تغييراً.
A law which I believe needs urgent reform is any statute that prevents whistleblowers from reporting institutional corruption without fear of prosecution. Those who expose wrongdoing should be protected, not punished.
What qualities should a good judge or lawyer have?
ما الصفات التي يجب أن يمتلكها قاضٍ أو محامٍ جيد؟
A good judge is someone who always prioritises evidence over emotion, who treats every defendant with equal dignity regardless of background, and who understands that the law exists to serve justice, not the other way round.
