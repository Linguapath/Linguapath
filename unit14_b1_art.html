الكتابة

اكتب عن الفن والإبداع
What form of art or creativity are you most passionate about?
ما شكل الفن أو الإبداع الذي تنجذب إليه أكثر؟
I am deeply passionate about photography and have been fascinated by it since childhood. I am particularly interested in capturing everyday moments that people overlook — the kind of beauty that results from paying close attention to ordinary life.
Do you think art should always have a social or political message?
هل تعتقد أن الفن يجب أن يحمل دائماً رسالة اجتماعية أو سياسية؟
I believe art is capable of carrying powerful social messages but should never be required to do so. Some of the most profound art results from a purely personal exploration with no political intent. I am opposed to the idea that art must justify itself through utility.
Describe an artwork, film, or piece of music that moved you deeply.
صف عملاً فنياً أو فيلماً أو قطعة موسيقية أثّرت فيك بعمق.
I was profoundly moved by Beethoven's Ninth Symphony the first time I heard it performed live. I was struck by the contrast between the darkness of the earlier movements and the overwhelming joy of the final chorus. I am still amazed that it was composed by someone who was completely deaf.
How has digital technology changed art and creativity?
كيف غيّرت التكنولوجيا الرقمية الفن والإبداع؟
Digital technology has resulted in a democratisation of art — anyone with a smartphone is now capable of producing and distributing creative work globally. However I am concerned about the impact of AI-generated art on human artists, and I think the debate about what counts as genuine creativity is far from over.
Should governments fund the arts? Why or why not?
هل يجب أن تموّل الحكومات الفنون؟ لماذا؟
I am strongly in favour of public arts funding. I am convinced that art is essential to a healthy society — it contributes to mental wellbeing, cultural identity, and critical thinking. Without government support, the arts become accessible only to the wealthy, which results in a much narrower cultural landscape.
