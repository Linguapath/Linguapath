الكتابة

اكتب عن تجاربك في بيئة العمل
Write a formal email opening to a client you have not met before.
اكتب افتتاحية بريد رسمي لعميل لم تلتقِ به من قبل.
Dear Mr Johnson, I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to introduce myself and to enquire about the possibility of scheduling a meeting at your earliest convenience to discuss a potential collaboration.
Describe your current role and responsibilities formally.
صف دورك الوظيفي ومسؤولياتك بأسلوب رسمي.
In my current position as Senior Content Manager, I am responsible for overseeing the development and delivery of all educational materials, managing a team of five writers, and ensuring all deliverables meet our quality benchmarks.
Write a polite way to decline a meeting or request.
اكتب طريقة مهذبة لرفض اجتماع أو طلب.
Thank you for your kind invitation. While I greatly appreciate the opportunity, I regret to inform you that I am unable to attend on that date due to a prior commitment. I would welcome the chance to reschedule at your convenience.
Write a sentence proposing a solution to a workplace problem.
اكتب جملة تقترح فيها حلاً لمشكلة في بيئة العمل.
Following our recent discussions, I would like to propose that we consider implementing a structured onboarding process which would address the issues raised and ensure all new team members are fully operational within two weeks.
Write a formal closing for a business email.
اكتب خاتمة رسمية لبريد إلكتروني تجاري.
I trust the above information is of use. Should you require any further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact me directly. I look forward to hearing from you. Yours sincerely.
