الكتابة — IELTS Writing Task 2

مقالات أكاديمية عن التقنية والمجتمع
IELTS · TASK 2

📋 Abstract Noun Phrases في IELTS Writing

  • بدل "AI is developing rapidly" → "The rapid development of AI"
  • بدل "governments fail to regulate technology" → "The failure of governments to regulate technology"
  • بدل "people widely use smartphones" → "The widespread adoption of smartphones"
  • تُعطي الكتابة كثافةً أكاديمية وتُمكّن من التعبير بإيجاز — علامة Band 7+
Artificial intelligence will do more good than harm. To what extent do you agree?
الذكاء الاصطناعي سيفيد أكثر مما يضرّ. إلى أي مدى توافق؟
The rapid development of artificial intelligence represents both the greatest opportunity and the most serious risk in contemporary technology. The case for optimism rests on concrete evidence: the application of AI to medical diagnosis has already demonstrated the ability to detect cancers earlier and more accurately than human specialists in controlled settings. The potential for reducing the burden of routine cognitive work, freeing humans for more creative and relational tasks, is real. Nevertheless, the failure to develop adequate regulatory frameworks alongside AI systems poses genuine dangers. The widespread automation of jobs — not only manual but cognitive — threatens to concentrate economic gains among those who own the technology while displacing millions. The inability of existing institutions to respond at the pace of technological change is perhaps the most pressing concern. On balance, I believe AI will do more good than harm — but only if the governance of its development keeps pace with its power.
Technology is increasing inequality rather than reducing it. Do you agree?
التقنية تُفاقم التفاوت بدلاً من تقليصه. هل توافق؟
The relationship between technological change and inequality is more complex than either optimists or pessimists typically acknowledge. The unprecedented growth of digital platforms has, in some respects, democratised access to information, markets, and services previously available only to the wealthy. At the same time, the concentration of ownership of these platforms in the hands of a very small number of individuals represents a new form of economic power whose scale has no historical precedent. The inability of tax systems designed for an industrial economy to capture the profits generated in a digital one means that the accumulation of technological wealth tends to occur largely outside existing redistributive mechanisms. The failure to invest in digital infrastructure in poorer regions deepens existing divides. On balance, I largely agree: the rapid deployment of new technologies, without deliberate policy to ensure broad access to their benefits, tends to increase rather than reduce inequality.
Should the development of artificial intelligence be regulated by governments? Discuss.
هل يجب أن تُنظّم الحكومات تطوير الذكاء الاصطناعي؟ ناقش.
The question is not whether AI should be regulated but how — and how quickly. The case against heavy regulation rests on the risk of stifling innovation: the imposition of premature constraints on a technology whose capabilities and applications are not yet fully understood could prevent beneficial uses from emerging. The case for regulation is, however, compelling. The development of autonomous weapons systems capable of making lethal decisions without human oversight raises questions no technology company is equipped to answer alone. The potential for the use of AI in surveillance — the mass monitoring of populations — demands accountability that markets cannot provide. The failure of self-regulation in social media, where algorithmic amplification of harmful content proceeded unchecked for years, offers a cautionary precedent. Effective AI governance requires not the suppression of development but the establishment of clear ethical boundaries, transparency requirements, and liability frameworks capable of adapting as the technology evolves.
People are becoming too dependent on technology. To what extent do you agree?
الناس يصبحون معتمدين على التقنية أكثر من اللازم. إلى أي مدى توافق؟
The growing reliance on digital tools for navigation, communication, memory, and social interaction raises legitimate concerns. The outsourcing of cognitive tasks to devices capable of performing them more efficiently than human minds weakens, over time, the muscles of memory, spatial reasoning, and mental arithmetic. The displacement of face-to-face interaction by screen-mediated communication has implications for social skills, particularly in children and adolescents. That said, dependency on tools is not new to human society. The invention of writing was, in a sense, an outsourcing of memory; the development of arithmetic notation changed how humans thought about number. What matters is not whether we use powerful tools but whether we retain sufficient understanding of our reliance on them to function when they fail. The widespread adoption of a single operating system, network, or platform creates systemic vulnerabilities whose scale is genuinely new — and genuinely concerning.
The benefits of space exploration outweigh the costs. To what extent do you agree?
فوائد استكشاف الفضاء تفوق تكاليفه. إلى أي مدى توافق؟
The exploration of space has generated technologies whose application on Earth — satellite navigation, water purification systems, medical imaging advances — has produced benefits vastly exceeding their original contexts. The development of international scientific collaboration required by space programmes has also generated diplomatic and cultural value that is difficult to quantify but real. The objection that resources invested in space exploration are better spent solving problems on Earth is serious but ultimately unconvincing. The ability of governments and private actors to pursue multiple priorities simultaneously means that space exploration does not preclude the funding of earthly needs. The growing privatisation of space, however, raises important questions: the extraction of resources from celestial bodies, the accumulation of orbital positions by private companies, and the militarisation of space are all scenarios demanding international governance frameworks. The exploration of space is worth pursuing — but only with appropriate oversight of who benefits and who bears the risks.
