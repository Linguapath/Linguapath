الكتابة

اكتب عن فن السرد والحكاية
Write the opening paragraph of a story set in a place you know well.
اكتب الفقرة الافتتاحية لقصة تدور في مكان تعرفه جيداً.
It was raining when she arrived at the station. She had been travelling for six hours and her feet were aching. The city she had grown up in looked different somehow — smaller, perhaps, or maybe she had simply grown. She stood on the platform, watching the other passengers disappear, and wondered what she had come back for.
Describe a turning point in a story — a moment when everything changed.
صف نقطة تحوّل في قصة — لحظة تغيّر فيها كل شيء.
He had been walking home when he saw the letter. It was lying on the doorstep — he would not have noticed it had the wind not blown it open. He picked it up. His hands were trembling. Everything he had believed about his family had just collapsed in a single sentence.
Write a paragraph describing a character through their actions, not their appearance.
اكتب فقرة تصف شخصية من خلال أفعالها لا مظهرها.
She would always arrive early to every meeting — not anxiously, but calmly, as if time itself owed her something. She never interrupted. She would listen until the other person had finished, then pause a beat longer than felt comfortable, and only then speak. When she did, nobody looked at their phone.
Write a paragraph using flashback — a character remembering a past event.
اكتب فقرة باستخدام الاسترجاع — شخصية تتذكر حدثاً ماضياً.
She closed her eyes. She was twelve again. They had been sitting on the roof of her grandmother's house, watching the city below. Her grandmother had been singing quietly. She had not understood the words then, but now, standing in the empty apartment, she could finally hear what the song had been saying.
Write the final paragraph of a story — a satisfying or unexpected ending.
اكتب الفقرة الأخيرة لقصة — نهاية مُرضية أو مفاجئة.
Years later, she would think about that morning and smile. Not because everything had worked out — it had not, not entirely — but because she had finally stopped waiting for it to. She had been walking along the river when it happened: the precise moment she had decided that enough was enough, and that a life half-lived was no kind of life at all.
