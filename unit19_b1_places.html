الكتابة

اكتب عن وصف الأماكن
Describe a city or place you have visited that left a strong impression on you.
صف مدينة أو مكاناً زرته وترك انطباعاً قوياً لديك.
Never have I been so immediately overwhelmed by a city as I was by Istanbul. Rarely does a place combine so many contrasts — ancient and modern, East and West, sacred and secular — in such a small geographical space. Only when I stood on the Galata Bridge did I fully grasp the city's extraordinary scale.
Describe the place where you grew up. What made it distinctive?
صف المكان الذي نشأت فيه. ما الذي جعله مميزاً؟
Rarely did a day pass without the smell of fresh bread from the bakery on the corner. Not only was the neighbourhood physically beautiful, but it had a remarkable sense of community that I have never found replicated elsewhere. Only in hindsight did I realise how profoundly that place shaped my sense of belonging.
Compare a rural and an urban environment. Which do you prefer and why?
قارن بين البيئة الريفية والحضرية. أيهما تفضل ولماذا؟
Never have I felt as genuinely at peace as I do in the countryside, yet rarely can I sustain that peace without the stimulation of city life. Not only does the city offer opportunity and diversity, but it provides the anonymity that creativity sometimes requires. Only by living in both have I come to appreciate each on its own terms.
Describe a place that has changed significantly since you first knew it.
صف مكاناً تغيّر بشكل ملحوظ منذ أن عرفته لأول مرة.
Not until I returned after ten years did I grasp how completely the neighbourhood had transformed. No longer did the small shops line the streets — they had been replaced by identical chain stores. Seldom had I felt such a profound sense of loss for a place that technically still existed.
Describe your ideal place to live. Use inversion to make your description vivid.
صف مكان إقامتك المثالي. استخدم القلب لجعل وصفك حيّاً.
Never would I choose a place without access to nature and water. Not only would it need to be beautiful, but it would have to offer genuine community. Rarely do I find both in the same location, yet only in such a place could I imagine truly flourishing.
