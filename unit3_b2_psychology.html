الكتابة — IELTS Writing Task 2

مقالات أكاديمية عن علم النفس والسلوك
IELTS · TASK 2

📋 الـ Subjunctive في IELTS Writing

  • استخدمه في الجسم: "It is essential that schools teach emotional resilience."
  • بعد recommend/suggest/insist/demand: "Experts recommend that patients undergo therapy."
  • مع wish/if only: "Many wish that stigma around mental health were gone."
  • قاعدة ذهبية: الـ subjunctive = base verb بعد that — بدون s، بدون was → were
Mental health issues are becoming more common. What are the causes of this, and what can be done?
مشاكل الصحة النفسية آخذة في التزايد. ما أسبابها وما الحلول؟ (Problem/Solution)
Mental health problems have grown significantly, and it is essential that we understand why before proposing solutions. Modern life demands that individuals be constantly productive, connected, and achieving — conditions that breed anxiety and depression. Social media, in particular, requires that users present idealised versions of themselves, deepening feelings of inadequacy. As for solutions, it is vital that governments invest in accessible therapy and that schools teach emotional resilience from an early age. It is also important that employers be required to provide mental health support. Above all, it is crucial that the stigma surrounding mental illness be dismantled — for without open conversation, few will seek the help they need.
Some argue that understanding psychology helps people become better leaders. To what extent do you agree?
هل الفهم النفسي يجعل الناس قادة أفضل؟ (Opinion)
It is widely agreed that effective leadership requires more than technical expertise — it demands that a leader understand what motivates people, how they respond to stress, and what conditions allow them to perform. I largely agree with this claim. Research insists that leaders who demonstrate empathy and self-awareness consistently achieve better outcomes than those who rely on authority alone. It is important, however, that we not overstate the case. Psychology equips leaders to ask better questions, but it does not require that they be trained therapists. The essential thing is that leaders remain curious about behaviour, open to feedback, and committed to the well-being of those they lead.
Some people believe that advertising manipulates consumer behaviour unfairly. Do you agree or disagree?
هل الإعلانات تتلاعب بسلوك المستهلكين بشكل غير عادل؟ (Opinion)
Modern advertising demands that marketers understand the psychology of desire, fear, and identity — and it would be naive to deny that this knowledge is sometimes deployed unfairly. I largely agree. Advertisements routinely exploit cognitive bias and emotional vulnerability, and it is essential that regulators require that advertising be clearly labelled as such. It is particularly important that children be protected from persuasion techniques they are not equipped to recognise. That said, not all advertising manipulates; some is genuinely informative. It is vital, however, that consumers be educated about the psychological strategies used against them — for awareness, even imperfect, is the best defence against manipulation.
Is human behaviour mainly determined by nature or by nurture? Discuss.
هل السلوك البشري يتشكّل أساساً من الطبيعة أم البيئة؟ (Discuss both views)
The debate between nature and nurture has occupied psychologists for over a century, and it is essential that we resist the temptation to resolve it too neatly. Those who favour nature argue that genetics requires that we acknowledge certain inborn traits — tendencies toward anxiety, aggression, or resilience that emerge regardless of environment. The nurture camp insists that society be recognised as the primary sculptor of identity, pointing to the profound effects of upbringing and culture. My view is that neither camp alone is adequate. It is vital that future research treat the two as interacting systems rather than competing explanations. What matters most is not which force is dominant, but how understanding both can help us build more supportive environments.
What are the advantages and disadvantages of social media for mental health?
ما مزايا وعيوب وسائل التواصل الاجتماعي للصحة النفسية؟ (Advantages/Disadvantages)
Social media's effects on mental health are genuinely mixed, and it is important that we resist both utopian and dystopian conclusions. On the positive side, online communities allow that isolated individuals be connected with others who share their experiences — particularly those managing chronic illness or navigating minority identities. Research suggests that when used intentionally, social media can reduce loneliness. The disadvantages, however, are also real. Algorithms demand that content be emotionally provocative to attract engagement, which rewards outrage and anxiety. It is crucial that platforms be required to make their recommendation systems transparent. Above all, it is essential that young users be equipped with the critical skills to engage without being overwhelmed.
