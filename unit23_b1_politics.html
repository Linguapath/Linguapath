الكتابة

اكتب عن السياسة والديمقراطية
What is it that makes democracy so difficult to maintain?
ما الذي يجعل الديمقراطية صعبة الحفاظ عليها؟
It is the combination of institutional weakness and public disengagement that makes democracy so fragile. What democracy requires is not just formal structures but a genuinely informed and active citizenry. It is disinformation, not foreign interference, that most threatens established democracies from within.
Is it possible for a government to be both effective and democratic?
هل يمكن لحكومة أن تكون في آنٍ واحد فعّالة وديمقراطية؟
It is the tension between speed and accountability that makes democratic governance challenging. What authoritarian systems offer is efficiency — at the cost of freedom. It is the Scandinavian democracies that demonstrate most convincingly that effectiveness and democratic accountability are not in conflict.
What is the biggest threat to democracy in the world today?
ما أكبر تهديد للديمقراطية في العالم اليوم؟
It is the rise of populism exploiting genuine economic grievances that most threatens democracy today. What makes this threat distinctive is that it operates through democratic mechanisms — elections, referendums, parliamentary procedure — while undermining the values those mechanisms were designed to protect.
Should voting be made compulsory? Argue your position.
هل يجب جعل التصويت إلزامياً؟ دافع عن موقفك.
It is low voter turnout that most weakens the legitimacy of democratic outcomes. What compulsory voting achieves is a more representative electorate, reducing the outsized influence of motivated minorities. It is Australia that demonstrates this system can work effectively without significant coercion.
What reforms would most improve politics in your country?
ما الإصلاحات التي ستُحسّن السياسة في بلدك أكثر؟
It is the funding of political parties by private interests that most distorts political decision-making. What the system needs is strict campaign finance reform. It is transparency in lobbying that would do most to restore public trust — not constitutional changes that are difficult to achieve.
