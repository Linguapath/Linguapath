الكتابة

اكتب عن مسيرتك المهنية
What is your current field or dream career?
ما مجالك الحالي أو المهنة التي تحلم بها؟
I currently work in education, which I find deeply rewarding. My dream career would involve combining teaching with content creation — I have been working towards this goal for the past two years.
What is your greatest professional achievement?
ما أكبر إنجاز في مسيرتك المهنية؟
My greatest achievement has been developing a language learning platform from scratch. I have been working on it consistently for over a year and I am proud of how much it has grown.
What is your professional ambition?
ما طموحك المهني؟
I have been working towards becoming a recognised expert in educational technology. I hope to have published my own learning resources and built a sustainable platform within the next five years.
