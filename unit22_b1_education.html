الكتابة

اكتب عن التعليم والتعلّم
Write about something you wish you had learned earlier in life.
اكتب عن شيء تمنيت لو تعلّمته في وقت أبكر من حياتك.
I wish I had been taught financial literacy at school. If only I had understood compound interest and investment principles in my twenties, I would have made very different decisions. I should have sought this knowledge independently rather than assuming school would provide it. Now I wish the curriculum were designed to prepare students for real adult life.
Describe your ideal learning environment. What would it include?
صف بيئة التعلّم المثالية. ماذا ستتضمن؟
I wish my school had offered more project-based learning. If only teachers had been allowed more freedom to design their own curricula rather than following rigid syllabuses. I should have pushed for more discussion-based classes. Ideally, I wish every student were assessed on skills development rather than memory retention alone.
Should university education be free? Argue your position.
هل يجب أن يكون التعليم الجامعي مجانياً؟ دافع عن موقفك.
I wish access to higher education were not determined by financial means. If only governments had invested more heavily in university funding decades ago, we would not be facing the current debt crisis. I should not have had to choose my degree based on future earning potential. Education should be a right, not a financial transaction.
What is the most important skill schools should teach that they currently do not?
ما أهم مهارة يجب أن تُعلّمها المدارس وهي لا تفعل ذلك حالياً؟
I wish critical thinking had been treated as a core subject rather than an incidental skill. If only schools had devoted time to teaching students how to evaluate evidence and identify logical fallacies, public discourse would be healthier. I should have been challenged more rigorously to question my assumptions. I wish the system were built around curiosity rather than compliance.
Describe a teacher who had a significant impact on you.
صف معلماً كان له تأثير كبير عليك.
I wish I had told her how much she changed my life before she retired. If only I had understood at sixteen what I understand now about how rare genuine teaching is. She made me wish I were a better thinker every single lesson. I should have worked harder to deserve her belief in me.
