الكتابة

اكتب عن الحجج وإبداء الآراء
Write your opinion on whether social media does more harm than good.
اكتب رأيك حول ما إذا كانت وسائل التواصل الاجتماعي تضرّ أكثر مما تنفع.
Although social media connects people across the world, it has undeniably contributed to rising rates of anxiety and depression, particularly among young people. Even though the benefits are real, the psychological costs appear to outweigh them. Despite arguments about freedom of expression, tighter regulation seems necessary.
Do you think economic growth is more important than environmental protection?
هل تعتقد أن النمو الاقتصادي أهم من حماية البيئة؟
Despite the urgent need for economic development, environmental destruction creates costs that far exceed any short-term gains. Although growth is necessary for reducing poverty, it is possible to pursue sustainable models. Even though many governments resist change, the evidence is overwhelming.
Should university education be free for everyone? Argue both sides.
هل يجب أن يكون التعليم الجامعي مجانياً للجميع؟ ناقش الجانبين.
Although free university education would reduce inequality, the financial burden on governments is substantial. Even though several countries have implemented it successfully, their contexts differ significantly. Despite the appeal of universal access, a mixed model of grants and low-interest loans may be more realistic.
Is it ever acceptable to break the law? Give your opinion with reasons.
هل يجوز أحياناً كسر القانون؟ أعطِ رأيك مع الأسباب.
Even though laws exist to maintain social order, history shows that unjust laws must sometimes be challenged. Although civil disobedience carries risks, it has driven some of the most important social progress in history. Despite this, I believe legal channels should always be exhausted first.
Do you think technology is making people less creative?
هل تعتقد أن التكنولوجيا تجعل الناس أقل إبداعاً؟
Although technology provides powerful creative tools, constant access to entertainment may be reducing our capacity for deep, original thought. Even though many artists use digital tools brilliantly, the habit of passive consumption is concerning. Despite these worries, I remain cautiously optimistic about technology's creative potential.
