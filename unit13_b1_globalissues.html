الكتابة

اكتب عن القضايا العالمية
What global issue concerns you most and why?
ما القضية العالمية التي تقلقك أكثر ولماذا؟
What concerns me most is global inequality. It is the widening gap between the richest and poorest nations that I find most alarming. What makes it particularly troubling is that this gap is growing despite decades of development aid and international agreements.
Do you think wealthy nations do enough to help developing countries?
هل تعتقد أن الدول الغنية تفعل ما يكفي لمساعدة الدول النامية؟
It is clear that wealthy nations could do significantly more. What they currently provide in foreign aid rarely matches what they extract through trade imbalances. It is fair trade and debt cancellation, not charity, that would make a real difference.
What role should international organisations like the UN play in solving global problems?
ما الدور الذي يجب أن تلعبه المنظمات الدولية كالأمم المتحدة في حل المشاكل العالمية؟
What the UN needs most is genuine enforcement power. It is the lack of binding mechanisms that undermines its effectiveness. What I would propose is a reformed Security Council with broader representation and the ability to sanction nations that violate international law.
How does poverty affect other global issues like health and education?
كيف يؤثر الفقر على قضايا عالمية أخرى كالصحة والتعليم؟
It is poverty that underlies most preventable deaths globally. What people in extreme poverty lack is not simply money but access — to clean water, vaccines, qualified teachers, and basic infrastructure. It is this cycle of deprivation that makes poverty so difficult to escape without systemic intervention.
What single change would most improve the world in the next twenty years?
ما التغيير الواحد الذي سيُحسّن العالم أكثر في العشرين سنة القادمة؟
What would most transform the world is universal access to quality education. It is education that breaks the cycle of poverty, reduces conflict, empowers women, and builds the informed citizenry that democracy requires. What is remarkable is that we already have the resources — what we lack is the political will.
