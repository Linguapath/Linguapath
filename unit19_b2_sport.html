الكتابة — IELTS Writing Task 2

IELTS · TASK 2

📋 Mixed Discourse Patterns في IELTS Writing

  • للإضافة: "Furthermore / Moreover / What is more — professional sport generates enormous economic activity."
  • للتباين: "By contrast / In contrast — amateur sport serves primarily social functions."
  • للنتيجة: "Consequently / As a result / Hence — doping undermines the integrity of competition."
  • للتسليم مع المعارضة: "Notwithstanding its benefits, professional sport raises serious ethical concerns."
  • للشرط: "In so far as sport promotes health, it deserves public investment."
Professional sport has become too commercialised. To what extent do you agree?
الرياضة الاحترافية أصبحت تجارية للغاية. إلى أي مدى توافق؟
Professional sport has always had a commercial dimension. Notwithstanding this, the scale and nature of commercialisation has changed so dramatically in recent decades that legitimate concerns arise. Broadcasting revenues, sponsorship agreements, and franchise valuations have transformed elite sport into a financial sector in its own right. Consequently, decisions that were once made on sporting grounds — fixture scheduling, player transfers, competition formats — are now made primarily on commercial ones. Furthermore, the financial gap between the wealthiest and poorest clubs in most sports has widened to the point where genuine competition is structurally compromised. By contrast, amateur and community sport — which serves the majority of actual participants — receives minimal investment. On balance, I agree that commercialisation has gone too far: not because profit is inherently wrong in sport, but because the financial model currently dominant prioritises spectacle over participation, and shareholder returns over sporting integrity.
Hosting international sporting events such as the Olympics brings more benefits than drawbacks. To what extent do you agree?
استضافة الأحداث الرياضية الدولية كالأولمبياد يُجلب فوائد أكثر من عيوبها. إلى أي مدى توافق؟
The question of whether hosting major sporting events benefits host nations is one on which the economic evidence has become considerably more sceptical in recent years. Notwithstanding the genuine intangible benefits — national pride, international visibility, and the stimulus to infrastructure development — the financial record of recent host cities tells a consistently sobering story. Consequently, the argument that hosting the Olympics or World Cup generates economic benefits must now be made with considerable qualification. Moreover, the pattern of cost overruns is systematic rather than exceptional: on average, the cost of summer Olympics has exceeded budget by over 150%. By contrast, the benefits — tourist revenue, international broadcasting fees — are often overstated. That said, in so far as hosting an event genuinely transforms infrastructure and stimulates long-term economic activity, the case can be made. The evidence suggests, however, that this outcome is the exception rather than the rule. I therefore largely disagree: the costs typically outweigh the benefits, and the resources could be better spent.
Physical education should be the most important subject in schools. To what extent do you agree?
التربية الرياضية يجب أن تكون أهم مادة في المدارس. إلى أي مدى توافق؟
The claim that physical education should be the most important school subject is one that most educators would find difficult to sustain against the competing demands of literacy, numeracy, science, and the humanities. Nevertheless, the health case for physical education is strong and underappreciated. Consequently, most developed systems currently underinvest in it relative to the evidence. Furthermore, the benefits extend beyond the physical: regular physical activity is consistently associated with improved mental health, better concentration, and enhanced academic performance in other subjects. Notwithstanding this, to call physical education the most important subject would be to oversimplify both the purpose of education and the specific contribution that physical activity makes. In so far as the claim means that physical education deserves significantly more time, resource, and status than it typically receives, I agree. Whether it deserves the top position in a hierarchy of subjects is a different question, and one that depends on contested values about what education is for.
Doping in sport should result in a lifetime ban. To what extent do you agree?
التنشيط المحظور في الرياضة يجب أن يُفضي لحظر مدى الحياة. إلى أي مدى توافق؟
Doping represents one of the most serious integrity threats facing elite sport. By contrast with other forms of rule-breaking, it is premeditated, systematic, and directly deceptive of other competitors, spectators, and sponsors. Consequently, the argument for severe sanctions is strong. Furthermore, a weak deterrent — a two-year ban, for instance — may be economically rational for an athlete who can earn far more in two years of enhanced performance than they would lose by serving it. Notwithstanding this, a lifetime ban raises proportionality concerns that deserve serious consideration. In so far as an athlete was young and subject to systemic pressure from coaches or national programmes, absolute individual responsibility is more complex than it first appears. Moreover, the evidence base for lifetime bans as a deterrent is not conclusive. On balance, I support longer and more consistent bans than currently exist, combined with stronger systemic accountability — but I would stop short of arguing that lifetime bans are categorically warranted in every case.
Sport can bring nations together and promote world peace. To what extent do you agree?
الرياضة يمكنها جمع الأمم وتعزيز السلام العالمي. إلى أي مدى توافق؟
The claim that sport can promote peace is not entirely unfounded, but it requires significant qualification. Notwithstanding the genuine instances in which sporting exchanges have facilitated diplomatic contact — most famously in the 'ping-pong diplomacy' between the USA and China in 1971 — the systemic evidence for sport as a peacebuilding force is considerably weaker than its proponents suggest. Furthermore, competitive sport can intensify rather than resolve national rivalries: the football match between El Salvador and Honduras in 1969 preceded a brief war between the two countries. By contrast, the view that sport is politically neutral — that it exists in a separate sphere from geopolitics — is equally hard to sustain. Notwithstanding this, in so far as sport creates opportunities for people from different backgrounds and nations to interact as human beings rather than as abstractions, it can contribute to the social conditions in which peace becomes more sustainable. Consequently, I would argue that sport can support peace at the margins — but only when embedded in broader political commitment to it.

🎙️ Discourse Markers في Speaking

  • "Furthermore, sport teaches you how to handle failure."
  • "By contrast, professional athletes have very different pressures from amateurs."
  • "Consequently, without proper investment, many young people lose access to sport."
  • "Notwithstanding the health benefits, sport can cause serious injuries."
  • "In so far as sport builds teamwork, it prepares people for work life."
