الكتابة

اكتب عن تجاربك الثقافية
Describe your cultural background and identity.
صف خلفيتك الثقافية وهويتك.
If I had not grown up between two cultures, I would not have developed the perspective I have today. My background has given me the ability to see situations from multiple angles, which I consider my greatest strength.
What cultural tradition do you value most and why?
ما التقليد الثقافي الذي تقدّره أكثر ولماذا؟
If this tradition disappeared, society would lose something irreplaceable. I value the tradition of communal meals, where families gather to share food and stories — it maintains bonds that modern life constantly threatens.
What cultural stereotype about your country would you challenge?
ما صورة نمطية عن بلدك تريد تحديها؟
If people knew the truth, they would realise that this stereotype is based on a very limited understanding of a complex society. My country has an extraordinary intellectual heritage that rarely receives international attention.
How has globalisation changed your culture in recent years?
كيف غيّرت العولمة ثقافتك في السنوات الأخيرة؟
If globalisation had not reached us so rapidly, we would still have a stronger connection to our local dialects and traditions. While it has brought opportunities, it has also accelerated cultural homogenisation.
If you could preserve one cultural aspect for future generations, what would it be?
لو حفظت جانباً ثقافياً واحداً للمستقبل ما هو؟
I would choose to preserve our storytelling tradition, because if it disappeared, future generations would lose their most direct connection to their ancestors' wisdom, values, and history.
