الكتابة

اكتب عن روتينك اليومي
Describe your morning routine.
صف روتينك الصباحي.
I invariably wake up at six thirty and almost always start my day with a glass of water before coffee. I tend to spend the first thirty minutes reading before checking my phone — a habit I have kept for years.
What are your work or study habits?
ما عاداتك في العمل أو الدراسة؟
I work best in the morning, so I nearly always tackle the most demanding tasks first. I rarely leave important work until the evening, and I consistently take short breaks every ninety minutes to maintain focus.
What do you do in the evenings to unwind?
ماذا تفعل في المساء للاسترخاء؟
I hardly ever stay up past midnight. I generally spend my evenings reading or going for a walk. I occasionally cook something new, which I find is a great way to switch off from screen time.
